I answer business questions with SQL, Excel and dashboards, and I show my working.

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in AI & Data Science who wants to work as a Data Analyst. I cover the full workflow: pulling data with SQL, cleaning and analysing it in Python, profiling it in Excel, and presenting the findings in Power BI, Tableau and Streamlit.

Across 7 projects I've compared conversion between an ad group and a control, forecast daily sales for 1,115 stores, analysed which telecom and credit card customers churn, built Power BI reports on retail sales and banking attrition, and scored sentiment on 568,454 product reviews. Two of them run as live apps.

What I do best is check the data before trusting it. I checked row counts after loading the A/B data into MySQL (all 588,101 users), turned blank competitor distances into NULL so they were not read as zero-distance rivals, and dropped two columns from the credit card churn file that were another model's churn predictions. Then I explain the result, including what the data can't support, in plain language.

The work I'm most sure of is SQL, cleaning data and turning the answer into a Power BI or Tableau dashboard. Forecasting, classification and text analysis sit alongside that, and I can explain how each one works. All seven projects use public datasets, so the next thing I want is real business data to work on.

Looking for: a Data Analyst internship or entry-level role, remote, hybrid or on-site. Available immediately.
