I answer business questions with SQL, Excel and dashboards, and I show my working.

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in AI & Data Science who wants to work as a Data Analyst. I handle the whole job: pulling and cleaning data with SQL and Excel, analysing it in Python, and presenting the findings in Power BI, Tableau and Streamlit.

Across 7 projects I've tested whether an ad lifts conversions, forecast daily sales for 1,115 stores, analysed why telecom customers churn, and scored sentiment on 568,454 product reviews. Two of them run as live apps.

What I do best is check the data before trusting it: I've caught a silent import error and a NULL-handling bug that were producing wrong numbers. Then I explain the result, including what the data can't support, in plain language.

Looking for: a Data Analyst internship or full-time entry-level role, remote, hybrid or on-site. Available immediately; I can manage my B.Tech alongside the role.
