03 / About

About

Turning raw data into decisions — then building the systems that keep those decisions running.

Student · Aspiring Data Analyst / Data Scientist

I'm a student and aspiring Data Analyst / Data Scientist interested in turning raw data into useful decisions and building systems around it. My work sits where analysis meets software.

Across my projects I've explored data analysis, machine learning, experimentation, natural-language processing, forecasting, dashboards, AI automation, and full-stack development — moving from exploratory analysis and predictive models to interactive dashboards and deployed applications.
