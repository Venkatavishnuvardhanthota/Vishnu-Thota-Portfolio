About

Data Science & Analytics

Student · Data Analyst / Data Scientist

I work at the intersection of data, analysis, and applied machine learning.

I'm a Data Science & Analytics student focused on turning raw data into structured insights, predictive models, dashboards, and useful applications. My work spans exploratory data analysis, cleaning and wrangling, statistical analysis, machine learning, natural-language processing, business intelligence, and visualization — and increasingly the application development that turns analysis into something people can actually use.

I'm drawn to practical problems: forecasting demand, testing a marketing change, predicting churn, reading sentiment at scale. Across these projects I own the work end to end, from raw data through models and dashboards to deployment — and two of them now run as live, public applications. I care about honest analysis and clear communication: making results understandable and useful for technical and non-technical audiences alike, without overstating what the data supports.
